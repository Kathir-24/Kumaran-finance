# Kumaran Chits — Statutory & Operational Forms Suite

> **Legal Reference**: **Tamil Nadu Chit Funds Rules, 1984** & **The Chit Funds Act, 1982** (Central Act No. 40 of 1982)  
> **Company**: **Kumaran Finance** (Head Office: Perundurai, Erode, Tamil Nadu)  
> **Location in Repo**: `sample-statutory-forms/`  
> **Visual Screenshots**: `sample-statutory-forms/screenshots/`

This directory contains the sample PDF files generated by the **Kumaran Chits** PDF canvas engine. It includes both the **Pre-filled forms** (showing how software automatically populates all database fields) and **Blank forms** (matching the blank templates in the official Government Gazette of Tamil Nadu Chit Funds Rules, 1984).

### Visual Styling & Layout Hardening
- **Bold Black Underlines (`#000000`, 1.2pt)**: Placed strictly where blanks exist in the original Gazette forms (Place, Date, Applicant names, father's/husband's name, firm name, fee amounts, sanction order dates, and signature lines). No superfluous underlines on static boilerplate text.
- **Bold Blue Entered Data (`#003399` / `Helvetica-Bold`)**: All software-entered details stand out clearly in bold blue against the black legal template.
- **Anti-Overlap Line Clearance & Generous Spacing**:
  - Underline y-offset is calibrated at `y + lineHeight(false) + 1.2pt`, placing the line safely below the lowest letter descenders (`g`, `j`, `p`, `q`, `y`, and Tamil subscripts `றீ`, `சு`, `ம்`).
  - Consecutive wrapped lines enforce `lineGap: 3.5`–`4pt`, guaranteeing that text above never intersects underlines and subsequent lines start with clean white-space clearance.
  - Eliminated all vertical coordinate collisions (From/To headers, dual-column signature blocks, and multi-line bilingual captions).
- **Blank Gazette Templates**: Clean bold black blank underscores (`____________________`) render in place of data, perfectly mimicking the unpopulated Government Gazette blanks without awkward wrapping or hardcoded values.
